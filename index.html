Welcome to our clubhouse!

Feel free to check out the bookshelves and grab a beanbag chair.

This is where we hold the meetings of our SECRET BOOK CLUB about how nerds can make the world a better place through art, design, health and democracy.

What's the secret? Every month, Alice and Avery talk about what we've read. Before the podcast begins, neither of us knows what book the other is reading. Once the beans are spilled, we search for connections between subjects we didn't see coming.


You can listen in on Spotify, on Apple Podcasts, or if you're fancy, you can add this RSS feed directly to your podcatcher: https://betternerdpod.github.io/BetterNerdPod.xml

If you want to listen right here on the website, check out our episodes page.


And since you're an official SECRET BOOK CLUB member, you can email us at BetterNerdPod@proton.me to tell us what connections you found between our reading and yours. Welcome to the possible nerds!

If you'd like to keep the possible nerds going, you can find us on Patreon.
